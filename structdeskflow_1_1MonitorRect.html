Detailed Description

One monitor in a computer's own coordinate space.

Coordinates are pixels. The rectangle is half-open: it covers x .. x+width-1 and y .. y+height-1. Two monitors touch when one rectangle's right/bottom edge equals the other's left/top edge (no gap and no overlap).
